## 1. Empowering AI data scientists using a multi-agent LLM
- **Summary**: BioMedAgent is a self-evolving LLM multi-agent framework that learns to use various bioinformatics tools and chain them into executable workflows for autonomously carrying out diverse biomedical analyses.
- **Link**: [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-026-01634-6](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-026-01634-6)

## 2. Conditional optimal transport for single-cell transcriptomes
- **Summary**: Driessen et al. present a conditional optimal transport method that can model the distribution shift between perturbed and unperturbed cell transcriptomes and that can generalize to unseen contexts.
- **Link**: [https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/articles?year=2026](https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/articles?year=2026)

## 3. Data-science challenges in therapeutic interventions
- **Summary**: An outline of key data-science challenges highlighting disease contexts in which intelligent systems could support therapeutic interventions.
- **Link**: [https://www.nature.com/nbt/articles?year=2026](https://www.nature.com/nbt/articles?year=2026)

## 4. Artificial intelligence in bioinformatics: a survey
- **Summary**: Discusses how AI techniques, particularly deep learning and reinforcement learning, have achieved groundbreaking advances in medical diagnostics, drug discovery, and genomic analyses.
- **Link**: [https://academic.oup.com/bib/article/26/6/bbaf576/8315885](https://academic.oup.com/bib/article/26/6/bbaf576/8315885)

## 5. Bioinformatics and artificial intelligence in genomic data analysis
- **Summary**: This review examines how artificial intelligence, particularly machine learning and deep learning, is transforming genomic data analysis and addressing critical limitations of traditional bioinformatics methods.
- **Link**: [https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00438-025-02314-x](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00438-025-02314-x)

## 6. Integrating artificial intelligence with bioinformatics promotes public health
- **Summary**: Highlights scientific efforts to promote public health by integrating AI with bioinformatics, focusing on toxicity prediction in drug development and chemical safety.
- **Link**: [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10798184/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10798184/)

## 7. Generative Artificial Intelligence in Bioinformatics
- **Summary**: A systematic identification and evaluation of generative AI approaches in genomics, proteomics, transcriptomics, structural biology, and drug discovery.
- **Link**: [https://arxiv.org/html/2511.03354](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.03354)

## 8. Bioinformatics Meets AI: Transforming Big Data into Breakthroughs
- **Summary**: Explores the convergence of bioinformatics and artificial intelligence, revolutionizing biotechnology by transforming biological big data into actionable insights.
- **Link**: [https://www.researchgate.net/publication/391194965_Bioinformatics_Meets_AI_Transforming_Big_Data_into_Breakthroughs_in_Biotechnology](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/391194965_Bioinformatics_Meets_AI_Transforming_Big_Data_into_Breakthroughs_in_Biotechnology)

## 9. AI and Machine Learning in Biology: From Genes to Proteins
- **Summary**: Provides an overview of cutting-edge AI methodologies spanning from neural networks to advanced transformer architectures and large language models for proteomic data interpretation.
- **Link**: [https://www.mdpi.com/2079-7737/14/10/1453](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-7737/14/10/1453)

## 10. HPC and AI in bioinformatics
- **Summary**: Discusses the success of AI in bioinformatics relying on high-performance computing power to open new possibilities in precision medicine and drug discovery.
- **Link**: [https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167739X25003140](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167739X25003140)
