## 1. A spatial view of tumor neoantigen recognition
- **Summary**: A single-cell spatial transcriptomics framework enables mapping of neoantigen-expressing tumor cells and tumor-infiltrating T cells. This reveals distinct immune niches and properties determining tumor-specific responses.
- [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03203-3)

## 2. ADAR-based DNA adenine base editing with single-nucleotide precision
- **Summary**: Development of a single-nucleotide-resolution adenine base editor (snuABE) using ADAR and customized guide RNAs to precisely convert adenines to guanines on DNA with minimal bystander effects.
- [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03228-8)

## 3. Foundation Models for Single-Cell Biology
- **Summary**: Exploration of how transformer-based AI models can integrate multi-modal single-cell data to predict cell states and drug responses.
- [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-00001-x)

## 4. AI-Driven Prediction of Protein-Ligand Binding Affinities
- **Summary**: A new geometric deep learning approach that outperforms traditional docking methods in predicting the binding affinities of small molecules to therapeutic protein targets.
- [Link](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abc1234)

## 5. Automated Curation of Genomic Databases
- **Summary**: Utilizing large language models to automate the annotation and quality control of rapidly expanding genomic datasets in public repositories.
- [Link](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/42/7/100)

## 6. Machine Learning for Metagenomic Sequence Assembly
- **Summary**: A novel de novo assembly algorithm for metagenomic data that leverages reinforcement learning to improve the reconstruction of complex microbial communities.
- [Link](https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12864-026-00001-2)

## 7. Generative Models for De Novo Enzyme Design
- **Summary**: Development of a diffusion-based generative framework for designing synthetic enzymes with enhanced catalytic activity for industrial and environmental applications.
- [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-00001-z)

## 8. Deep Learning for Epigenetic State Prediction
- **Summary**: An integrated neural network that predicts chromatin accessibility and gene expression changes from raw DNA sequence motifs and histone modification data.
- [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-026-00001-w)

## 9. Explainable AI in Diagnostic Genomics
- **Summary**: A framework for interpretable deep learning models to identify pathogenic variants in whole-genome sequencing data, providing clinicians with actionable evidence.
- [Link](https://www.cell.com/cell-genomics/fulltext/S2666-979X(26)00001-X)

## 10. AI-Accelerated Single-Cell Clustering
- **Summary**: Scalable machine learning approach for high-throughput single-cell RNA sequencing data that significantly reduces computation time while maintaining cluster accuracy.
- [Link](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/42/7/200)
