## 1. Global Trends and Hotspots of Genetic Research on Non-syndromic Cleft Lip with or Without Cleft Palate: A Bibliometric and Bioinformatics Analysis

Bibliometric analysis of genetic research on non-syndromic cleft lip with or without cleft palate, identifying global trends, hotspots, and key research patterns. This study provides insights into the worldwide distribution and evolution of cleft lip research.

- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42928946/)

## 2. Unveiling A Dual-pathway Prognostic Model: Integrating T Cell-related and Ubiquitination-related Genes in Lung Adenocarcinoma

Integrative bioinformatics analysis identifying a dual-pathway prognostic model combining T cell-related and ubiquitination-related genes for lung adenocarcinoma prediction and treatment guidance.

- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42935957/)

## 3. GTranslate: Rapid and Accurate Translation Table Prediction for Prokaryotic Genomes

Computationally efficient tool gTranslate that uses an ensemble of five machine learning methods to accurately predict translation tables for prokaryotic genomes, enabling improved genome annotation and analysis.

- [PeerJ](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.21825)

## 4. Extracting Candidate Motif Patterns for Multiple RNA Modifications Using Direct RNA Sequencing

Application of exponential population analysis to direct RNA sequencing data from HEK293T cells and peripheral human blood to identify candidate motif patterns for pseudouridine (Ψ), inosine (I), and 5-methylcytosine (m⁵C).

- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42934531/)

## 5. Computational Mueller Matrix Microscopic Pathology Based on Differential Decomposition for Medical Reference Mapping

Physics-informed computational framework termed Computational Mueller Matrix Microscopic Pathology, based on differential Mueller matrix decomposition for medical reference mapping and optical pathology.

- [Frontiers in Bioengineering](https://doi.org/10.3389/fbinf.2026.1953765)

## 6. Identification of Genes Associated with Gut Microbiota Metabolites in Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus Complicated By Acute Cerebral Infarction Based on Bioinformatics and Mendelian Randomization

MR analysis identifying 12 candidate microbial taxa shared by both Type 2 Diabetes and acute cerebral infarction, using the FinnGen diabetes endpoint as a proxy for T2DM-related genetic susceptibility.

- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42940176/)

## 7. Genomic Insights Into Population Structure, Resistome, Virulome, and Mobilome of Multidrug-resistant Pseudomonas Aeruginosa

Assessment of the interplay between population structure, antimicrobial resistance determinants, virulence factors, and mobile genetic elements in multidrug-resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa genomes from public databases.

- [Frontiers in Microbiology](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2026.1940877)

## 8. Evolutionary Conservation and Regulatory Characteristics of SF3B3 in Plants

Comprehensive analysis of plant SF3B3 genes using comparative genomics, phylogenetics, gene structure and motif analysis, alternative splicing profiling, promoter analysis, expression assays, subcellular localization, and protein interaction validation.

- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42901314/)

## 9. MolMMP: Structure-aware Cross-modal Alignment with Geometry-biased Attention for Molecular Toxicity Prediction

Novel multimodal framework MMB-DTI that synergistically fuses evolutionary and chemical semantics, extracted via pre-trained language models (ESM-2 and ChemBERTa), with structural topologies derived from graph neural networks for molecular toxicity prediction.

- [Frontiers in Bioengineering](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12859-026-06745-0)

## 10. AbLWR: A Context-Aware Listwise Ranking Framework for Antibody-Antigen Binding Affinity Prediction Via Positive-Unlabeled Learning

Novel framework AbLWR that reformulates the conventional antibody-antigen binding affinity regression task as a listwise ranking problem to directly optimize candidate prioritization using positive-unlabeled learning.

- [Bioinformatics](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics_btag793)
