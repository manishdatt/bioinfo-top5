## Top Bioinformatics Articles - Week 25

1. **Engineering AI co-scientists for statistical genetics applications**
   * Summary: Explores how AI co-scientists act as virtual research collaborators in statistical genetics, accelerating discovery through robust domain-specific data and infrastructures.
   * [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-025-02487-6)

2. **Universal restoration of medical images**
   * Summary: Introduces HorusEye, a self-supervised foundation model that learns realistic noise to enable robust tomography restoration across diverse modalities without clean training data.
   * [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43588-026-00975-1)

3. **Structural variant calling using Sniffles2**
   * Summary: Details an open-source software for reliable detection of structural variants (50 bp+) from long-read sequencing across various allele fractions.
   * [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41596-026-01362-w)

4. **When the genome learned its own vocabulary**
   * Summary: Discusses DeepSEA, a pioneering deep convolutional neural network for predicting functional effects of non-coding variants, and its influence on computational genomics.
   * [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41576-026-00966-y)

5. **MethylVI: Deep generative model for single-cell bisulfite sequencing**
   * Summary: Enhances analyses of single-cell methylomic data by accounting for unique technical and biological sources of variability.
   * [Link](https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/research-articles)

6. **Causal circuit tracing in single-cell foundation models**
   * Summary: Reveals distinct computational architectures in single-cell models, including inhibitory dominance and biological coherence.
   * [Link](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/advance-articles)

7. **Statewide multi-year wastewater sequencing reveals dual origins of HIV-1 signal**
   * Summary: Uses wastewater genomics and computational approaches to track HIV-1 and distinguish community signals from lentiviral vector contamination.
   * [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-74140-7)

8. **A lipidomics roadmap: from basic research to societal challenges**
   * Summary: Outlines advances and future directions in lipidomics at the interface of biology and analytical chemistry.
   * [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-73797-4)

9. **On the importance of being powerful to detecting specific regulations in omics studies**
   * Summary: Argues that low statistical power in many omics studies leads to false interpretations of specific biological responses.
   * [Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41477-026-02303-x)

10. **Artificial intelligence in bioinformatics: a survey**
    * Summary: Provides a broad overview of AI techniques like deep learning and reinforcement learning transforming medical diagnostics and drug discovery.
    * [Link](https://academic.oup.com/bib/article/26/6/bbaf576/8315885)
