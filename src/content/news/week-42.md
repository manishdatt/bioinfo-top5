## 1. Panoramic modelling of single-cell transcriptomes at scale with CellNet

Xiaoqing Lian, Pei Liu, Jiahui Tan, Luoda Tan, Chaoyi Li, Quan Zou, Chen Lin, Xiangxiang Zeng. arXiv:2610.21943. A method for panoramic modelling of single-cell transcriptomes at scale, enabling comprehensive analysis of cellular diversity and state transitions across large datasets.

- [arXiv:2610.21943](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21943)

## 2. OmicsBench: a long-horizon multi-turn benchmark of AI agents on research-study-scale bioinformatics

Gideon Shaked, Siddharth Nagisetty. arXiv:2610.21200. A benchmark for evaluating AI agents on bioinformatics research tasks, covering multi-step reasoning, tool use, and experimental design across omics domains.

- [arXiv:2610.21200](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21200)

## 3. Metabolic model repair via quotient-space exploration

 Novel approach for genome-scale metabolic model repair that collapses equivalent repairs into canonical mechanisms and optimizes exploration over the quotient space, improving distinct successful-core discovery.

- [arXiv:2610.11627](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11627)

## 4. MD-LLM-2: A Transferable Language Model of Molecular Dynamics with Physical Conditioning and Explicit Path Probabilities

Molecular dynamics language model that generates trajectories conditioned on sequence, temperature and molecular history, with explicit path likelihoods and transferable across protein domains.

- [arXiv:2610.10879](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10879)

## 5. Toward Reliable Patient-Specific Aortic Strain Mapping from 4D CTA: Validation, Spectral Structure, and Clinical Potential

Framework for spatially resolved ascending aortic strain estimation from 4D computed tomography angiography, characterized across segmentation sources and Laplace-Beltrami spectral scales.

- [arXiv:2610.10913](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10913)

## 6. Elucidating the Space of Enzymatic Reaction: A Unified Benchmark and Pretrained Model

VenusRX-Bench and VenusRX: unified benchmark and T5-style model for enzymatic reaction prediction, retrosynthesis, and EC-number prediction, bridging chemical and enzymatic domains.

- [arXiv:2610.11694](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11694)

## 7. Neural Decoding as Cognitive Inference

Recasting neural decoding as cognitive inference constrained by brain-intrinsic priors, yielding meta-neural semantic representations that enable recovery of stable cognitive states from variable neural observations across five neural recording modalities.

- [arXiv:2610.11923](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11923)

## 8. La-Ribo: RNA Co-Design via Geometry-Latent Flow Matching

Generative framework for RNA sequence-structure co-design via geometry-latent flow matching, with a quality-controlled corpus of 168,561 RNA structures and improved designability over baselines.

- [arXiv:2610.12236](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12236)

## 9. If the brain were so simple: Information-based drivers of intelligence and their interaction through an n-body-inspired framework

Framework proposing information enthalpy as a metric for neural systems, with an n-body-inspired model describing how distinct mechanisms interact to govern neural motion through high-dimensional state-space, giving rise to adaptive behaviors.

- [arXiv:2610.11142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11142)

## 10. Cross-species representation learning aligns mouse and human neural dynamics and tracks clinical drug efficacy

Dual-rule contrastive learning framework that aligns corresponding mouse and human neural states, recovering conserved sensory-response structure and resolving species-specific drug efficacy relationships in epilepsy.

- [arXiv:2610.11222](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11222)
