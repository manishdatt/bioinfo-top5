## 1. Association of Sex Hormone-Binding Globulin with Glycated Hemoglobin and Multidimensional Biomarker Structure in Reproductive-Age Women

ArXiv preprint enabling faster Bayesian inference for stochastic biochemical systems without requiring expensive simulation steps. This method accelerates parameter estimation and model selection for reaction-diffusion processes in cellular signaling.

- [arXiv:2610.37675](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37675)

## Affective Valence as the Interoceptive Rate of Change of Jacobian Scale

Explores how interoceptive signals tracking Jacobian scale changes can quantify emotional valence. Bridges computational neuroscience and affective psychology with quantitative measurements of brain state dynamics.

- [arXiv:2610.38075](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38075)

## PyGPLA: A Python Package for Generalized Phase Locking Analysis

Python software package for computing generalized phase locking values from multichannel neural and biosignal data. Provides standardized analysis pipeline for assessing cross-frequency coupling in electrophysiological recordings.

- [arXiv:2610.38236](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38236)

## The resource cost of cooperative invasion

Analyzes the metabolic and fitness costs associated with cooperative behavior in expanding biological populations. Uses evolutionary game theory and spatial modeling to quantify invasion trade-offs in microbial and cellular systems.

- [arXiv:2610.38394](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38394)

## Accurate quantification of protein concentrations and state populations by 2D 1H-N Q-BEST NMR

Introduces Q-BEST NMR methodology for precise protein concentration measurements and conformational state population analysis without requiring reference standards. Applicable to membrane proteins and challenging biomolecular systems.

- [arXiv:2610.38564](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38564)

## Spontaneous brain-wide rotational dynamics constrain cue-evoked behavior

Reports large-scale brain dynamics observations showing intrinsic rotational flows modulate behavioral responses to sensory cues. Combines fMRI and electrophysiology to link spontaneous activity patterns with decision-making processes.

- [arXiv:2610.38615](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38615)

## Spontaneous Spirals in Spatial Systems

Several chemical and biological systems produce spontaneous spirals in their dynamical behavior, such as the Belousov-Zhabotinsky reaction in chemistry and the slime mold Dictyostelium in biology. In a previous study, such spiral wave formation was accurately replicated with a computational spatial system known as the Ideal Storage Cellular Automaton Model (ISCAM).

- [arXiv:2610.38710](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38710)

## Open-Source Framework for the Visualization, Quantification, and Analysis of 4D Flow and High-Dimensional Magnetic Resonance Images

Import 4D Flow reads vendor DICOM, applies preprocessing, and stores velocities as a time-resolved vector field; Analyze 4D Flow provides measurement planes, streamline and Doppler-like visualization, segmentation propagation, and quantification. Underlying both, we extended the Insight Toolkit to store five- and higher-dimensional arrays.

- [arXiv:2610.38948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38948)

## Comparison of anomaly detection and supervised learning for acute myeloid leukemia blast identification: A proof-of-concept study

Compares six computational approaches for blast identification: four anomaly detection algorithms (Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM, Local Outlier Factor, Half-Space Trees), a supervised Random Forest classifier, and an unsupervised UMAP-HDBSCAN clustering approach.

- [arXiv:2610.39447](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39447)

## AI-driven structural informatics reveals diverse glycosylation machinery across double-stranded DNA viruses

Integrates structural-alphabet-guided searches and structure similarity networks with genomic-neighbourhood and biosynthetic gene cluster embedding analyses to examine viral GT-A proteins. This approach reveals extensive structural diversity overlooked by sequence-based annotation, including previously uncharacterized viral GT lineages.

- [arXiv:2610.39662](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39662)
