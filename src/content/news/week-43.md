## 1. Simulation-free approximate Bayesian computation for stochastic reaction networks

ArXiv preprint enabling faster Bayesian inference for stochastic biochemical systems without requiring expensive simulation steps. This method accelerates parameter estimation and model selection for reaction-diffusion processes in cellular signaling.

- [arXiv:2610.26361](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26361)

## 2. Affective Valence as the Interoceptive Rate of Change of Jacobian Scale

Explores how interoceptive signals tracking Jacobian scale changes can quantify emotional valence. Bridges computational neuroscience and affective psychology with quantitative measurements of brain state dynamics.

- [arXiv:2610.26350](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26350)

## 3. PyGPLA: A Python Package for Generalized Phase Locking Analysis

Python software package for computing generalized phase locking values from multichannel neural and biosignal data. Provides standardized analysis pipeline for assessing cross-frequency coupling in electrophysiological recordings.

- [arXiv:2610.26304](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26304)

## 4. The resource cost of cooperative invasion

Analyzes the metabolic and fitness costs associated with cooperative behavior in expanding biological populations. Uses evolutionary game theory and spatial modeling to quantify invasion trade-offs in microbial and cellular systems.

- [arXiv:2610.26235](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26235)

## 5. Accurate quantification of protein concentrations and state populations by 2D 1H-N Q-BEST NMR

Introduces Q-BEST NMR methodology for precise protein concentration measurements and conformational state population analysis without requiring reference standards. Applicable to membrane proteins and challenging biomolecular systems.

- [arXiv:2610.26017](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26017)

## 6. Spontaneous brain-wide rotational dynamics constrain cue-evoked behavior

Reports large-scale brain dynamics observations showing intrinsic rotational flows modulate behavioral responses to sensory cues. Combines fMRI and electrophysiology to link spontaneous activity patterns with decision-making processes.

- [arXiv:2610.25061](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25061)

## 7. Aptamers as a sandbox for lab-in-the-loop agentic molecular discovery

Describes how DNA/RNA aptamers can be engineered through iterative in vitro selection coupled with machine learning guidance. Enables discovery of high-affinity binders for diagnostics, therapeutics, and biosensor development.

- [arXiv:2610.24806](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24806)

## 8. Longitudinal fMRI reveals multi-network reconfiguration and a recurrent frontoparietal shift across rest and task states during adult aging

Longitudinal fMRI study tracking functional network reconfiguration patterns across aging. Identifies a consistent frontoparietal shift that accompanies the transition from rest to task states in older adults.

- [arXiv:2610.24433](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24433)

## 9. Spaceflight-associated Motion Sickness in Commercial Crew Astronauts

Investigates the prevalence and correlates of motion sickness among astronauts during spaceflight. Correlates symptoms with mission parameters, physiological measures, and individual susceptibility factors.

- [arXiv:2610.24216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24216)

## 10. LiveCoMS Roundtable: Discussing Markov state models after two decades of development

Comprehensive review of Markov state model (MSM) methodology for molecular dynamics simulations. Evaluates current best practices, limitations, and future directions for MSM applications in protein folding and conformational analysis.

- [arXiv:2610.24144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24144)
