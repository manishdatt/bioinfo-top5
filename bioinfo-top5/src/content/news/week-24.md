# Top 10 Bioinformatics Articles - Week 24

1. **Empowering AI data scientists using a multi-agent LLM**
   - **Summary:** BioMedAgent is a self-evolving LLM multi-agent framework that learns to use various bioinformatics tools and chain them into executable workflows for autonomously carrying out diverse biomedical analyses.
   - **Link:** [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-026-01634-6](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-026-01634-6)

2. **Generalist biological artificial intelligence in modeling the cell**
   - **Summary:** This review discusses the promises and pitfalls of biological AI algorithms and presents a vision for generalist biological artificial intelligence, in which models can perform diverse tasks across different biological domains.
   - **Link:** [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03064-w](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03064-w)

3. **DA-BioNER: data augmentation based on few-shot learning and distant supervision for biomedical named entity recognition**
   - **Summary:** This paper proposes a new method for biomedical named entity recognition (BioNER) using data augmentation, few-shot learning, and distant supervision, addressing data scarcity in specialized biological fields.
   - **Link:** [https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/advance-articles](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/advance-articles)

4. **Advances in Single-Cell AI Analytics**
   - **Summary:** An exploration of how deep learning architectures are being tailored to process high-throughput single-cell transcriptomics, improving resolution and cell-type classification.
   - **Link:** [https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/bioinformatics/articles/10.3389/fbinf.2025.1715821/full](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/bioinformatics/articles/10.3389/fbinf.2025.1715821/full)
