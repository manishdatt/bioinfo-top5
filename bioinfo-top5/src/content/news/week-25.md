## 1. Generalist biological artificial intelligence in modeling the 'language of life'
- **Summary:** This article explores how generalist biological artificial intelligence (GBAI) is transforming our ability to model the flow of information from DNA to cellular function, providing new insights into the 'language of life'.
- **Link:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03064-w

## 2. Artificial intelligence in bioinformatics: a survey
- **Summary:** A comprehensive survey of AI techniques (machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning) in bioinformatics, covering applications in protein structure prediction, gene regulation, and single-cell analysis.
- **Link:** https://academic.oup.com/bib/article/26/6/bbaf576/8315885

## 3. Causal circuit tracing reveals distinct computational architectures in single-cell foundation models
- **Summary:** This paper investigates the internal computational architectures of single-cell foundation models, focusing on inhibitory dominance, biological coherence, and cross-model convergence.
- **Link:** https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/advance-articles

## 4. Artificial intelligence in drug discovery
- **Summary:** A review of recent methods and systems for utilizing artificial intelligence to streamline and enhance the drug discovery pipeline.
- **Link:** https://www.nature.com/nbt/articles?year=2026

## 5. Bioinformatics and artificial intelligence in genomic data analysis
- **Summary:** An examination of how modern AI, especially deep learning, is overcoming the limitations of traditional bioinformatics in large-scale genomic data analysis.
- **Link:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00438-025-02314-x
