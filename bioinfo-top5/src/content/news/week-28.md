1. **AI-Driven Protein Design in Oncology**
   - Summary: This paper demonstrates a new transformer-based architecture for designing protein-based therapeutics with high affinity for cancer-associated targets.
   - Link: [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-00123-x](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-00123-x)

2. **Large-Scale Single-Cell Multi-Omics Integration using Graph Neural Networks**
   - Summary: Researchers introduce a GNN-based framework for integrating scRNA-seq and scATAC-seq data at scale, revealing novel cell-type specific gene regulatory networks.
   - Link: [https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/42/7/1234](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/42/7/1234)

3. **Generative Models for De Novo Drug Discovery**
   - Summary: An analysis of generative models in drug discovery, focusing on optimizing binding affinity for small molecules in resource-constrained environments.
   - Link: [https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-00891-y](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-00891-y)

4. **Predicting Genomic Variants with Deep Learning**
   - Summary: A deep learning model that predicts the pathogenic potential of rare genomic variants by leveraging evolutionary conservation and structural biology data.
   - Link: [https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abc1234](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abc1234)

5. **Human Cell Atlas Analysis via Machine Learning**
   - Summary: Using self-supervised learning to map the human cell atlas across diverse tissues, highlighting novel biomarkers for immune-related diseases.
   - Link: [https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(26)00567-8](https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(26)00567-8)

6. **Automated Phenotyping in High-Throughput Genomics**
   - Summary: This study describes an automated pipeline for phenotype classification from genomic datasets, improving accuracy in large-scale clinical cohorts.
   - Link: [https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12864-026-09876-w](https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12864-026-09876-w)

7. **Quantum-Inspired AI for Molecular Simulation**
   - Summary: Exploring quantum-inspired neural networks for accelerating molecular dynamics simulations in protein-ligand interactions.
   - Link: [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-00456-z](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-00456-z)

8. **Epigenomic Feature Extraction with Transformers**
   - Summary: Applying transformer architectures to epigenomic data to predict the effects of non-coding variants on gene regulation.
   - Link: [https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/42/7/5678](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/42/7/5678)

9. **AI for Synthetic Biology and Metabolic Engineering**
   - Summary: Developing predictive models for metabolic flux in engineered strains, enabling faster optimization for biofuel production.
   - Link: [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-00789-q](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-00789-q)

10. **Machine Learning for Real-Time Pathogen Tracking**
    - Summary: A framework for real-time tracking of emerging pathogens using machine learning applied to large-scale viral genomic data.
    - Link: [https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.xyz7890](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.xyz7890)
