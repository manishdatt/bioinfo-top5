# Weekly Bioinformatics Articles - Week 29

1. **Biotech's digital twin dilemma**
   - Summary: This article explores the promise and challenges of using digital twins for forecasting disease and guiding drug development, emphasizing the role of simulation models.
   - Link: https://www.nature.com/nbt/articles?year=2026

2. **The need for cell-cell interaction data in AI foundation models**
   - Summary: A perspective piece highlighting that cell-cell interaction data is a crucial missing layer for understanding complex biology and improving current AI foundation models in biology.
   - Link: https://www.talk.bio/2026/07/new-nature-biotechnology-article.html

3. **AlphaGenome: AI for understanding the genome**
   - Summary: Introduces a unifying DNA sequence model that advances regulatory variant-effect prediction and enhances our understanding of genome function.
   - Link: https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-ai-for-better-understanding-the-genome/

4. **UniCoracle: Automated hierarchical Feature Selection**
   - Summary: Describes a new automated hierarchical feature selection method using the UniCorP algorithm and Coracle machine-learning framework.
   - Link: https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/advance-articles

5. **AI and genomics: Transforming data analysis**
   - Summary: A comprehensive review on how AI, machine learning, and deep learning are addressing limitations in traditional bioinformatics for genomic data analysis.
   - Link: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00438-025-02314-x

6. **Generative models for protein design**
   - Summary: Research on how generative AI models are enabling the creation of novel functional proteins for therapeutic and biotechnological applications.
   - Link: https://www.nature.com/subjects/computational-biology-and-bioinformatics/ncomms

7. **Graph neural networks for metabolic network analysis**
   - Summary: Application of graph neural networks to predict metabolic pathway activity and identify potential drug targets in complex cellular environments.
   - Link: https://www.nature.com/subjects/computational-biology-and-bioinformatics/nature

8. **Single-cell multi-omics integration via deep learning**
   - Summary: A new deep learning approach to integrate single-cell transcriptomics and epigenomics, providing a more comprehensive cellular state mapping.
   - Link: https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/advance-articles

9. **AI-driven prediction of drug-target binding affinities**
   - Summary: Evaluation of advanced AI architectures in accurately predicting binding affinities between small molecules and novel therapeutic targets.
   - Link: https://www.nature.com/nbt/articles?year=2026

10. **Automated literature mining for biological knowledge graphs**
    - Summary: Use of natural language processing to extract and structure biological knowledge from vast quantities of scientific literature into cohesive knowledge graphs.
    - Link: https://link.springer.com/subjects/bioinformatics
